# 📒 XRPL Technical Courses

University of Nicosia (UNIC) aims to provide educational support in bridging the gap between technical education and hands-on experience through the recently launched [XRPL Next-Gen](https://xrpl.org), an initiative established with the objective to foster the growth and development of blockchain and FinTech education amongst tomorrow’s business leaders and developers.

The course aims to:

* educate developers on XRPL's ecosystem
* expose participants to XRPL's development tools and SDKs
* explore XRPL’s multi-currency network and logic
* acknowledge the mission and actions of the XRPL Foundation
