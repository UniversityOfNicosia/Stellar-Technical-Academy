<summary>Marios Touloupou, PhD</summary>

![](../../.gitbook/assets/touloupos.jpg)

_**Senior Researcher**_

**Dr. Marios Touloupou** is a senior researcher at the Institute for the Future (IFF) at the University of Nicosia, Cyprus. He has a robust background in digital systems, underpinned by his academic accomplishments and professional experiences in emerging technologies such as 5G/SDN networks and distributed ledger technologies (DLTs).

He began his academic journey by obtaining a B.Sc. degree in Digital Systems from the Department of Digital Systems of the University of Piraeus. His diploma thesis, titled “Three-Dimensional Web Platform for Multimedia Data Management (Applying the Approach to the Data of the Holy Sepulcher Restoration Project),” showcased innovative mechanisms that leverage “Data as a Service” technology to integrate big data management into cloud environments.

Dr. Touloupou continued his studies at the University of Piraeus, where he earned his master’s degree with a focus on “Advanced Information Systems & Services.” His master’s thesis was on “5G and V&V: Estimation of Performance in 5th Generation Network Services – Targeted Validation and Verification Tests,” highlighting his growing expertise in next-generation network technologies.

From 2017, Dr. Touloupou was actively involved in research at the Research Center of the University of Piraeus. His work, particularly focused on 5G/SDN networks, not only delved into the technological aspects but also considered their application in real-life scenarios, utilizing innovative tools to meet the needs of the ICT industry. He has contributed to research projects within the framework of EU Programmes, such as 5GTANGO, WindSider, MobiSpaces, Atlantis, Fame, with research interests spanning data management throughout the software life cycle and the enhancement of service quality through the monitoring of resources in fully distributed systems.

Dr. Touloupou’s current research at IFF has pivoted to distributed ledger technologies (DLTs), participating in the Ripple research program—an open-source, peer-to-peer decentralized platform that facilitates seamless money transfers in various forms, from traditional currencies to cryptocurrencies like litecoin or bitcoin. 

Having successfully defended his PhD thesis on “Assessing the Performance of Blockchain Protocols: Proposing and Validating a Blockchain Benchmarking Framework (BBF) through Controlled Deployments of XRPL and Ethereum,” Dr. Touloupou’s work at the University of Nicosia has significantly contributed to the field of decentralized technologies. His research focuses on enhancing the efficiency, security, and scalability of consensus algorithms in decentralized networks, marking a notable advancement in blockchain technology.
